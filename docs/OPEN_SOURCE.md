# Coreza Open Source Philosophy

Coreza is built openly to make institutional-grade trading automation accessible to individuals and teams everywhere. The platform is dual-licensed:

- **Community edition (AGPL-3.0)**: Full source code with the requirement that network-accessible modifications remain open.
- **Commercial license**: For organizations that need closed-source modifications, premium features, or enterprise support.

## Our Principles

1. **Security-first transparency**: Credential handling, encryption, and policy logic stay auditable.
2. **Community velocity**: Shared workflows, connectors, and risk controls improve faster when developed together.
3. **Sustainability**: Commercial offerings fund long-term maintenance, security reviews, and roadmap execution.
4. **Interoperability**: APIs, SDKs, and connectors remain open so teams can integrate Coreza into any stack.

## Contribution Expectations

- Follow the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Keep changes consistent with AGPL-3.0 obligations when distributing networked services.
- Mark paid-gated features clearly and document their configuration flags.

## Commercial Licensing

If your team needs private forks, custom SLAs, or expanded permissions beyond AGPL-3.0, review the [Coreza Commercial License](../COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md) and reach out to discuss terms.
